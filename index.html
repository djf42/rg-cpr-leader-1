CPR Team Leader Challenge

You're the team leader. Direct your team through a cardiac arrest using the RECOVER CPR algorithm: assign roles, add monitoring, read the rhythm at each pause, and give the right drugs at the right doses.

How it works

  • Tap a teammate and choose a task from the pop-up menu to give an order. Tasks take time, and each person does one thing at a time.
  • The timer at the top of the screen will help you track your 2-minute cycles. Once the team has completed all tasks, you can click Advance to 1:50 to fast-forward to 10 seconds before the end of the cycle; the game then slows down to real time for those last 10 seconds. During the fast-forward, your teammates will finish any assigned tasks and the monitor will keep updating. Click Normal speed if you want to slow back down to real time at any point during the fast-forward.
  • Click All eyes on the ECG if you want all members of the team to look at the monitor.
  • Click Pause compressions anytime you would like the compressor to pause.
  • Use the RECOVER dosing chart to find the correct doses for drugs and defibrillation, and enter those values when you give an order to a team member.

Prototype: Scenario 1 only. Scores aren't saved yet. Coached mode shows hints during the case.
